The best movies of all time

My Top 3 movies of all time

1. The Shawshank Redemption (1994) - Directed by Frank Darabont,

This film tells the story of two imprisoned men who form a deep friendship over the years.

2. The Godfather (1972) - Directed by Francis Ford Coppola,

This film tells the story of the powerful Italian-American crime family of Don Vito Corleone.

3. The Dark Knight (2008) - Directed by Christopher Nolan,

This film tells the story of Batman's battle with crime in Gotham City.
